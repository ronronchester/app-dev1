## 🎬 My Favorite Movie: *Pee Mak*

**Pee Mak** (2013) is a Thai horror-comedy film directed by Banjong Pisanthanakun. It retells the famous Thai legend of **Mae Nak Phra Khanong** with a lot of humor.

> "A love story that even death can't stop."

### 💡 Why I Like It

1. It balances **scary** moments and **funny** moments perfectly.
2. The story is based on a *well-known legend*.
3. The friendship of the characters is entertaining.
4. The ending is emotional.
